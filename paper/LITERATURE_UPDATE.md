# Literature update used in the manuscript

The manuscript literature was refreshed around three connected streams: agricultural multiobjective decision support, matheuristic production planning, and surrogate-assisted evolutionary optimization.

## Agricultural decision support

- Burbano-Figueroa, O., Sierra-Monroy, A., David-Hinestroza, A., Whitney, C., Borgemeister, C., & Luedeling, E. (2022). Farm-planning under risk: An application of decision analysis and portfolio theory for the assessment of crop diversification strategies in horticultural systems. *Agricultural Systems, 199*, 103409. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agsy.2022.103409
- Vergara Rodriguez, C. J., Mendoza-Ortega, G. P., Marmol Barriosnuevo, M., & Diaz Sierra, M. (2025). Multi-objective optimization for the sustainable planning of yam cultivation and distribution in the Colombian Caribbean. *Frontiers in Sustainability, 6*, 1653365. https://doi.org/10.3389/frsus.2025.1653365
- Huang, Z., Pu, Y., Zhang, Q., Wang, Y., Yang, J., & Gu, Y. (2025). A Fuzzy-Expert enhanced NSGA-II approach for sustainable agricultural systems. *Scientific Reports, 15*, 34070. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-14488-w
- Arismendi Joaqui, M. C., Henao Villabon, A. J., & Paredes Rodriguez, A. M. (2026). Multi-objective optimization of the sustainable fruit crop planning problem with a focus on small Colombian producers. *Kuwait Journal of Science, 53*(3), 100596. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.kjs.2026.100596
- Kropp, I., Nejadhashemi, A. P., Jha, P. K., Spinner, E., & Toscano Pulido, G. (2026). Integrating interactive decision making into evolutionary multiobjective agricultural optimization. *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, 242*, 111359. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2025.111359

## Surrogate-assisted evolutionary optimization

- Liu, S., Wang, H., Peng, W., & Yao, W. (2024). Surrogate-assisted evolutionary algorithms for expensive combinatorial optimization: A survey. *Complex & Intelligent Systems, 10*, 5933-5949. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40747-024-01465-5
- Pierce, C. M., Kim, Y.-K., & Bazarov, I. (2025). A comparison-relationship-surrogate evolutionary algorithm for multi-objective optimization. *Swarm and Evolutionary Computation, 95*, 101947. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.swevo.2025.101947
- Wang, Q., Li, H., Zhang, W., Zhang, Y., Gong, D., Chu, F., Mohamed, A. W., & Muhammad, I. (2026). Surrogate-assisted evolutionary algorithm with adaptive local region search for high-dimensional expensive multi-objective optimization problems. *Swarm and Evolutionary Computation, 100*, 102232. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.swevo.2025.102232

These references are used to position the study as a production-decision paper whose methodological contribution lies in the controlled comparison of exact multiobjective optimization, standard NSGA-II, full mathematical refinement, and surrogate-gated H-NSGA-II.
