# Literature update and positioning record

The literature revision organizes twenty-six verified and manuscript-used references into four streams matching the production-planning, agricultural, matheuristic, and surrogate-allocation arguments. Every bibliography entry supports an explicit nearby claim, while `LITERATURE_AUDIT.csv` records its DOI, verified source, stream assignment, and precise inclusion rationale.

## Positioning decisions

The revised Introduction now follows six argumentative stages covering decision significance, modern production-planning complexity, agricultural specificity, computational alternatives, three research questions, and bounded contributions. The Related Research section now uses the four requested subsections and concludes with an unheaded synthesis that states the integrated evidence gap without overstating algorithmic novelty.

The central novelty claim is comparative and integrative rather than foundational, because the study combines weekly agricultural scheduling, covariance-based price risk, status-aware exact references, full refinement, and surrogate-gated refinement. The surrogate is described as an allocator of true mathematical-refinement calls, because it neither substitutes reported objective values nor determines archive membership from predictions.

## Scope controls

Computational results, mathematical constraints, parameter values, tables, figures, and benchmark outputs were not changed during this literature update. One short Discussion paragraph was added solely to align the empirical interpretation with the narrower literature gap and to distinguish controlled refinement allocation from invention of a new optimization family.

Sources were excluded whenever publisher metadata or DOI resolution could not be verified confidently, including tempting topical papers whose bibliographic details remained incomplete. The final set therefore favors traceable journal records over a larger but less defensible bibliography, and every retained reference appears in the manuscript text.
