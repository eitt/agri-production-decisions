# Literature gap map

| Literature stream | What the verified literature establishes | What remains unresolved for this study | Manuscript response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Production planning under uncertainty, heterogeneous resources, and multiple objectives | Production systems increasingly require heterogeneous-resource models, competing objectives, exact methods, evolutionary search, and hybrid solution designs. | Existing production reviews do not determine how limited mathematical-improvement effort should be allocated among candidates within weekly agricultural schedules. | The paper compares exact reference computation, evolutionary search, exhaustive refinement, and selective refinement under common data and evaluation indicators. |
| Non-continuous resource use and inserted idle time | Dynamic lot sizing, scheduling taxonomies, integrated planning, and energy-aware scheduling show that zero production or inserted inactivity can be rational under specific economic or operational conditions. | Cross-industry evidence does not classify agricultural inactivity or establish that manufacturing effects transfer empirically to crop planning. | The decoder distinguishes mandatory agronomic inactivity from endogenous waiting, unused slots, and reduced cultivated area, while post-hoc diagnostics avoid automatic inefficiency labels. |
| Agricultural production planning under economic and biological constraints | Agricultural models connect maturation, harvest, uncertainty, diversification, environmental exposure, sustainability, and interactive multiobjective decision support. | Existing crop-planning studies rarely combine weekly lot occupancy, empirical local eligibility, crop-family recovery, continuous areas, and correlated harvest-price exposure. | The formulation integrates these elements while explicitly limiting uncertainty to covariance-based output-price exposure. |
| Matheuristics for multiobjective production planning | Production matheuristics exploit formulations, decomposition, neighborhoods, and mathematical subproblems to extend practical computational scale. | Prior applications seldom isolate whether every evolutionary candidate requires continuous optimization or whether selected calls preserve acceptable frontier quality. | Full and surrogate-gated variants use the same conditional continuous subproblem, allowing refinement allocation to become the controlled comparison. |
| Surrogate-guided allocation of optimization effort | Surrogate research studies expensive evaluations, model management, candidate relationships, local regions, and combinatorial search under restricted budgets. | Most methods predict objectives, constraints, or rankings rather than the realized gain obtained by granting mathematical refinement. | The surrogate predicts realized refinement gain, while selection, archive membership, and reported objectives continue using true evaluations. |

The defensible gap concerns the controlled connection among these streams, because none of the verified studies combines all five within one status-aware agricultural experiment. The manuscript therefore claims comparative and integrative novelty while avoiding claims that covariance risk, NSGA-II, matheuristics, surrogate models, or purposeful inactivity are individually new.
