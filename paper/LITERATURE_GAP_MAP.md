# Literature gap map

| Literature stream | What the verified literature establishes | What remains unresolved for this study | Manuscript response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Production planning under uncertainty, heterogeneous resources, and multiple objectives | Production systems increasingly require heterogeneous-resource models, competing objectives, exact methods, evolutionary search, and hybrid solution designs. | Existing production reviews do not determine how limited mathematical-improvement effort should be allocated among candidates in a weekly agricultural schedule. | The paper compares exact reference computation, evolutionary search, exhaustive mathematical refinement, and selective refinement under common data and indicators. |
| Agricultural production planning under economic and biological constraints | Agricultural models connect maturation, harvest, uncertainty, diversification, environmental exposure, sustainability, and interactive multiobjective decision support. | Existing crop-planning studies rarely combine weekly lot occupancy, empirical local eligibility, crop-family recovery, continuous areas, and correlated harvest-price exposure. | The formulation integrates these elements while explicitly limiting its uncertainty claim to covariance-based output-price exposure. |
| Matheuristics for multiobjective production planning | Production matheuristics exploit formulations, decomposition, neighborhoods, and mathematical subproblems to extend practical computational scale. | Prior applications seldom isolate whether every evolutionary candidate needs continuous optimization or whether selected candidates can preserve acceptable frontier quality. | Full refinement and surrogate-gated refinement use the same conditional continuous subproblem, enabling a direct allocation comparison. |
| Surrogate-guided allocation of optimization effort | Surrogate research studies expensive evaluations, model management, candidate relationships, local regions, and combinatorial search under restricted budgets. | Most methods predict objectives, constraints, or candidate rankings rather than the realized gain from granting a candidate mathematical refinement. | The surrogate predicts realized refinement gain, while all selection, archive membership, and reported objectives continue using true evaluations. |

The defensible gap concerns a controlled connection among these streams, because none of the verified studies combines all four elements within one status-aware agricultural experiment. The manuscript therefore claims comparative and integrative novelty, while avoiding claims that covariance risk, NSGA-II, matheuristics, or surrogate models are individually new.
