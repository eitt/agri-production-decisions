# Reference and policy audit

The bibliography was rechecked on 29 September 2026 by resolving every one of its thirty-one DOI identifiers through Crossref and comparing normalized titles, author surnames, publication years, journals, volumes, pagination or article numbers, and DOI strings against the manuscript entries. Every DOI resolved successfully, and every cited work remains traceable to its publisher record.

The audit corrected the Hu et al. (2024) title to the publisher-registered wording, “A matheuristic-based multi-objective evolutionary algorithm for flexible assembly jobs shop scheduling problem in cellular manufacture.” The remaining normalized title comparisons matched, apart from typographic equivalences involving the Greek epsilon character, capitalization, diacritics, ampersands, or dash styles.

Crossref returns online-first years for Chugh et al., Kucukkoc et al., Li et al., Qin et al., Rădulescu et al., and Seyfi and Yanıkoğlu, while the manuscript uses their final issue years. Publisher records confirm that those issue-year choices correspond to the stated volumes and page ranges, so they were retained rather than replaced by online-first dates.

The 2024–2026 records received additional publisher-level checks because their metadata were most likely to have changed before issue assignment. Their retained journal, volume, article-number or page-range, author, year, and DOI details agree with the current publisher or indexing records available on the audit date.

Taylor & Francis editorial guidance was also checked on 29 September 2026. The manuscript now contains a dedicated funding declaration, a competing-interest disclosure, CRediT-compatible author contributions, and a generative-AI declaration naming the tool and version, explaining its uses and limits, confirming author verification, and assigning full responsibility to the authors. Before submission, both authors should confirm that these declarations accurately describe their funding and tool use, because those facts cannot be inferred from repository contents alone.
